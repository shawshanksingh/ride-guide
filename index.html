Before you ride
  • Open this page in Chrome and add it to your home screen (⋮ → Add to Home screen).
  • Open the route once on Wi-Fi so it's saved on your phone.
  • During the ride, turn on Pocket mode and don't press the power button. The screen stays on but black, so GPS and audio keep running.
  • Use one earbud, or keep the volume low, so you can still hear traffic.
