HOW TO UPLOAD
GitHub → shawshanksingh/ride-guide → Add file → Upload files → drag in grunewald-lakes-and-villas.json and index.json → Commit.
Then on the phone open the app, close it, open it again.

ROUTE SUMMARY
Name: Lakes & Villas: Schöneberg → Grunewald
Distance: 7.8 km (about 35 min at 15 km/h)
Shape: one-way, ends at S Grunewald (S7) beside the Track 17 memorial
Theme: Grunewald villa colony, lakes and Weimar-era history

Stories:
1. Prager Platz (Kästner, Nabokov, the Prager Diele)
2. Church on Hohenzollernplatz ("God's Power Station")
3. Fehrbelliner Platz
4. Preußenpark and the Thai Meadow
5. Paulsborner Bridge, the Ringbahn and the lost Luna Park
6. Max Planck's house, Wangenheimstraße 21
7. Hubertussee and the Grunewald lake chain
8. Rathenau memorial stone, Koenigsallee
9. Hasensprung (the Hare's Leap) and Felix Koenigs
10. The Grunewald villa colony
11. Platform 17 memorial, Grunewald station

Validated with the app's nav.js + OSRM bike router: 7,755 m, all waypoints snapped within 19 m, no U-turns or detour legs, all stories within 90 m of the route (Hubertussee about 115 m, using a trigger on the route), at least 250 m apart.
