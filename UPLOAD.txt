HOW TO UPLOAD
GitHub → shawshanksingh/ride-guide → Add file → Upload files → drag in tiergarten-lions-and-lanterns-loop.json and index.json → Commit.
Then on the phone open the app, close it, open it again.

ROUTE SUMMARY
Name: Lions, Lanterns and Lakes
Distance: 9.9 km (about 40 minutes at 15 km/h)
Shape: Loop from and back to Bamberger Str. 8
Theme: Parks & lakes – the green heart of the Tiergarten
Note: three very short push sections (Corneliusbrücke, Löwenbrücke footbridge, Luiseninsel path)

Stories (10):
 1. Keithstraße and a Scottish field marshal (James Keith)
 2. Neuer See – Lenné's lake
 3. Löwenbrücke – Berlin's oldest suspension bridge, reopened July 2025
 4. Gas Lamp Open-Air Museum
 5. The English Garden – 1952 British gift, Anthony Eden, the burned teahouse
 6. Rousseau Island (and the Lortzing monument)
 7. Luiseninsel – Queen Louise's island
 8. Richard Wagner Monument
 9. The Japanese and Italian embassies
10. Karl-Heinrich-Ulrichs-Straße
