HOW TO PUBLISH
GitHub → shawshanksingh/ride-guide → Add file → Upload files → drag in gliders-trams-and-cadets-to-lichterfelde.json and index.json → Commit.
Then on the phone open the app, close it, open it again.

NOTE: index.json also keeps the five routes from 10–15 October that are in your local folders but not yet on GitHub
(botanic-garden-and-potsdam-road-loop, rebels-murals-and-the-wall, hasenheide-and-bohemian-rixdorf,
templars-and-garden-city-loop, domes-minarets-and-village-churches-loop). Upload their JSON files too if you haven't yet.

ROUTE SUMMARY
Name: Gliders, Trams and Cadets
Distance: 14.6 km (validated bike route), about 1 hour at 15 km/h
Shape: one-way, from Bamberger Str. 8 to S Lichterfelde Ost (S25, S26 + regional trains)
Theme: Lichterfelde's pioneers — Carstenn's villa suburb, the cadet academy, the Teltow Canal, Otto Lilienthal and the world's first electric tram (1881)
Stories:
  1. Nikolsburger Platz
  2. Bundesplatz
  3. Gritznerstraße
  4. The Fichtenberg weather tower
  5. Altensteinstraße
  6. Lichterfelde West station
  7. Carstenn's villa colony
  8. The Main Cadet Academy
  9. Drakestraße
  10. Lichterfelde manor and village church
  11. The Teltow Canal
  12. The Lilienthal memorial
  13. Lilienthal's home, Boothstraße 17
  14. The world's first electric tram
