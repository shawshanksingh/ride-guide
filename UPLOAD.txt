HOW TO UPLOAD
GitHub → shawshanksingh/ride-guide → Add file → Upload files → drag in writers-friedenau-loop.json and index.json → Commit.
Then on the phone open the app, close it, open it again.

ROUTE SUMMARY
Name: Writers' Friedenau: Benjamin to Benn Loop
Distance: 11.9 km (about 50 minutes at 15 km/h)
Shape: Loop from and back to Bamberger Str. 8
Theme: Literary Berlin - writers' homes in Wilmersdorf, Friedenau and Steglitz
Stories (12):
  1. Prinzregentenstraße: synagogue and Walter Benjamin
  2. Max Frisch, Sarrazinstraße 8
  3. Niedstraße: Grass, Johnson and Kommune One
  4. Kurt Tucholsky, Bundesallee 79
  5. Franz Kafka, Grunewaldstraße 13
  6. Kafka's first room, Muthesiusstraße
  7. The Bierpinsel
  8. Feuerbachstraße station
  9. Rosa Luxemburg, Cranachstraße 58
  10. Ceciliengärten
  11. Carl Zuckmayer Bridge
  12. Gottfried Benn, Bozener Straße 20
