ROUTE SUMMARY
Name:   Airlift Trail: Schöneberg → Tempelhofer Feld
Length: 9.5 km (bike route), about 40 minutes at 15 km/h
Shape:  One-way — ends at S+U Tempelhof (S41, S42, S45, S46, U6)
Theme:  Allied Berlin & aviation — the Airlift, Tempelhof airport and Tempelhofer Feld
Note:   Tempelhofer Feld is only open sunrise to sunset (gates close in the evening).

Stories:
  1. Winterfeldtplatz
  2. Kammergericht & Kleistpark
  3. Old St Matthew's Churchyard
  4. Kreuzbergstraße
  5. Viktoriapark waterfall
  6. Airlift Memorial
  7. Tempelhof Airport
  8. Columbia concentration camp memorial
  9. Tempelhofer Feld
  10. The Candy Bomber
  11. Orville Wright on the field

Validation: all 12 waypoints snapped within 7 m, no U-turns, no detour legs, POIs 6–76 m from the route and at least 420 m apart.
Note: index.json keeps the two pinned routes exactly as in your 2026-09-27 folder (with pinned/shape/created fields).
