Route summary
Name: Power & Stone: Schöneberg → Gendarmenmarkt
Distance: 6.5 km (about 26 minutes at 15 km/h)
Shape: one-way, ending at U Hausvogteiplatz (U2)
Theme: architecture and power - from Kaiser-era Berlin through the Nazi ministries and the Wall to Schinkel's square

Stories:
1. Wittenbergplatz station (Grenander, London roundel)
2. Villa von der Heydt
3. The Bendlerblock and 20 July 1944
4. The Philharmonie
5. Potsdamer Platz traffic-light tower
6. Detlev Rohwedder House (Air Ministry, 17 June 1953)
7. Niederkirchnerstraße, the Gestapo site and the Wall
8. Checkpoint Charlie
9. Gendarmenmarkt and Anton-Wilhelm-Amo-Straße
10. Hausvogteiplatz and the Jewish fashion quarter
