HOW TO UPLOAD
GitHub → shawshanksingh/ride-guide → Add file → Upload files → drag in courage-and-canals-ploetzensee-loop.json and index.json → Commit.
Then on the phone open the app, close it, open it again.

ROUTE SUMMARY
Name: Courage and Canals: the Plötzensee Loop
Distance: 14.7 km (about 1 hour at 15 km/h)
Shape: Loop from Bamberger Str. 8 and back
Theme: Resistance and remembrance, with canals and industry in north-west Charlottenburg and Moabit

Validated with the app's own nav.js + OSRM bike router: 14,732 m, all waypoints snapped within 23 m, no U-turn cues (ramp hairpins at the Spree bridge and the Goerdelersteg are covered by cue overrides), all POIs within 40 m of the route or given an on-route trigger, at least 370 m apart.

Stories (10):
1. Theater des Westens (Kantstraße)
2. Ernst-Reuter-Platz and the Telefunken tower
3. Physikalisch-Technische Reichsanstalt (Rubens, Planck and the birth of quantum physics)
4. Charlottenburg power station (across the Spree)
5. Goerdelersteg — Carl Goerdeler
6. Allotments, the Paul-Hertz estate and the resistance street names
7. Plötzensee Memorial (up Hüttigpfad on your left)
8. Westhafen (from the Beusselstraße canal bridge)
9. Levetzowstraße deportation memorial
10. Femina-Palast (Nürnberger Straße)
