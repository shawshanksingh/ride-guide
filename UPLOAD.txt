ROUTE SUMMARY
Name: Expressionists, Currywurst and the Devil's Mountain
Distance: 13.4 km (about 55 min at 15 km/h)
Shape: one-way, ending at S Heerstraße (S3, S9)
Theme: Weimar culture and the Cold War, heading west through Wilmersdorf and Halensee to the Teufelsberg
Note: the last 4 km are forest tracks with a climb up the Teufelsberg and some gravel; one short walking stretch at the Rathenauplatz crossing.

Stories (14):
1. Fasanenplatz
2. Pariser Straße 21 and 22 (Ignaz Friedman; Charlotte Kaletta and Fritz Pfeffer)
3. Moriz Seeler and the Lotto House
4. The Friedenstempel synagogue
5. Der Sturm, Katharinenstraße 5
6. Concrete Cadillacs on Rathenauplatz
7. Herta Heuwer and the currywurst
8. The Eichkamp settlement
9. Mommsenstadion
10. Drachenberg, Kite Mountain
11. The Devil's Mountain (Teufelsberg rubble)
12. Teufelssee
13. Field Station Berlin
14. The listening post today
