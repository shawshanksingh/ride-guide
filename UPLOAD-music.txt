NEW MUSIC STYLE: TRIBAL TECHNO

GitHub → shawshanksingh/ride-guide → Add file → Upload files → drag in these 5 files:
  music.js, app.js, index.html, sw.js, README.md
→ Commit. (Don't upload the .mp3, it's only a preview for you.)
Then on the phone open the app, close it, open it again (sw.js has a new version number, so the phone fetches the new files).

HOW TO USE IT
Open any route → Settings → Music style → "Tribal techno (124 bpm)". The choice is remembered.
"Test voice" plays 10 seconds of the chosen style. You can also switch styles mid-ride; it swaps straight over.

WHAT IT PLAYS
Generated live on the phone, like the ambient music, so there are no audio files and no copyright issues.
- Four-on-the-floor kick, offbeat open hats, a swung shaker
- Three toms tuned to the route's key, playing rolling patterns that change every 8 bars
- A conga line in 3-against-4 over the beat
- A rolling offbeat bass, claps and rimshots
- Arrangement: percussion-only intro → kick → bass → full groove → breakdown with a filter sweep → full groove, then it keeps cycling with new tom patterns. A tom fill leads into each new section.
- Loudness is matched to the ambient style. It dips under the voice more quickly and more deeply than the ambient does, so the directions stay clear.

Tested in Chrome: rendered and measured, switched live between the two styles, and the setting survives a reload. No errors.
